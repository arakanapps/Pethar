# Péthar (ဖေ့သာ) Product Principles

## Mission

**အာရက္ခပြည်သူများအတွက် ငွေပေးချေမှု၊ ငွေလွှဲမှုနှင့် နိုင်ငံတကာဘဏ္ဍာရေး ဆက်သွယ်မှုများကို လုံခြုံ၊ ရိုးရှင်းပြီး ခေတ်မီ ဒစ်ဂျစ်တယ်စနစ်ဖြင့် ဖြေရှင်းရန်။**

Empower the people of Arakan with resilient digital financial infrastructure. Péthar eliminates reliance on scarce, worn, or deprecated legacy fiat currencies by instituting the **Arakan Dollar (AKD)**, backed 100% by liquid reserves and gold.

## Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. Currency Policy: The Arakan Dollar (AKD)
- **Unit of Account**: Arakan Dollar (**AKD** / **₳** / **အာရက္ခဒေါ်လာ**), divisible into 100 Cents (ပြား).
- **Reserve Safeguarding**: 1:1 parity anchor backed by segregated liquid reserves (US Treasury bills, audited physical gold bullion, and regulated stablecoins) with zero fractional-reserve lending.
- **Complete Legacy Currency Deprecation**: Legacy non-circulating currencies are excluded from the Arakan ledger.

### 2. Domestic Mobile Money (bKash / TrueMoney / KPay Benchmark)
- **Peer-to-Peer (P2P)**: Free instant transfers using Phone numbers, Péthar IDs (`ARAK-XXXX`), or Arakan QR (AK QR) codes.
- **Physical Agent Network**: Verified branch counters across Sittwe, Mrauk-U, Kyaukpyu, Thandwe, Ramree, Minbya, and Ponnagyun.
- **Dynamic Cash-Out Security**: Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP) refreshed every 30 seconds to prevent unauthorized withdrawals.

### 3. Banking & Corridors (Wise / Payoneer / Card Rails)
- **Global Receiving Account**: Multi-currency receiving accounts for global freelance, corporate payroll, and remittances with SWIFT code `ARAKMMXX`.
- **Card Top-Up Gateway**: Simulated 3D-Secure gateway for Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.
- **Péthar Debit Card**: Virtual and physical Visa-compatible cards with instant in-app freeze/unfreeze controls.

### 4. Crypto & Fiat Stablecoin Bridge (Binance / MEXC / Multi-Chain)
- **Direct Exchange Bridges**: 0% fee integration via Binance Pay and MEXC Global UID.
- **Multi-Chain USDT & USDC**: TRC-20, ERC-20, TON, and Polygon deposit enclaves.
- **Instant Swap Engine**: Guaranteed 1:1 swap between USDT and AKD with transparent On-Chain Proof of Reserves (105% coverage).

### 5. Point of Sale (POS) Merchant Terminal
- Touchscreen register for local retailers, pharmacies, fuel stations, and grocery markets across Arakan.
- Real-time Arakan QR (AK QR) generation and simulated Bluetooth thermal receipt printing.

### 6. AI Cyber Defense Shield & Blackout Resilience
- **Zero-Trust Append-Only Ledger**: Cryptographically chained transactions with Ed25519 signatures.
- **AI Threat Neutralization**: Real-time anomaly detection, velocity checks, and brute-force deterrents.
- **Offline Mesh Protocol**: Enables signed offline vouchers during regional internet and telecom blackouts, safely reconciling on reconnect.
- **Coercion / Duress Protection**: Secret Duress PIN (e.g. 999999) displays a decoy balance and silently alerts community safety channels.

### 7. Bilingual Experience & Identity
- First-class support for **အာရက္ခ (Arakanese)** and **English**, accessible with one tap.
- Prominent integration of the sacred **Arakan Royal Red Emblem** (Srivatsa, sun, crescent moon, and crown) and flag.
