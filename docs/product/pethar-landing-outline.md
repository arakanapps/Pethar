# pethar landing page outline pethar.co.uk)

## Design references
- **Local market feel (Myanmar and Thai wallets and bank apps):** MTB (mtb.com.mm), KBZPay (kbzpay.com), K PLUS (Kasikornbank), Bangkok Bank Mobile Banking, TrueMoney, Tron Thai Next, and Xmoney.
  - Cues used: a row of quick actions at the top (Scan, Pay, Transfer, Top-up), a grid of service icons, a large balance card, QR scanning in the centre, rotating promo banners, live exchange rates, a licence and trust statement, and bilingual copy.
- **Global fintech polish:** Binance (binance.com) for a bold single headline, one clear call to action, product rows, and trust badges. Alipay for the super app "mini-apps" grid and its clean, airy spacing.
- **Global payments references:**
  - **Stripe:** clean, generous layouts, with subtle gradients that suit the glass look, plus developer-grade trust signals (uptime, security, compliance).
  - **Cash App:** one bold idea per screen, big amounts and short verbs (Send, Request, Pay), and a personal handle for each user.
  - **PayPal:** a familiar, reassuring payments tone, buyer-protection style promises, and simple "send money to anyone" messaging.
  - **PayPay (Japan):** a QR-first wallet with a big central Scan button, merchant cashback campaigns, and points.
- **Singapore banking references:**
  - **DBS / UOB / OCBC:** polished Southeast Asian banking apps, with calm layouts, clear spending insights, and a premium yet approachable tone.
  - **DBS PayLah! / POSB:** an everyday local wallet and neighbourhood-bank feel, with friendly copy, quick pay to phone numbers and QR, and simple savings.
-pethar's liquid glass look sits on top of all of this.
- Xmoney and Tron Thai Next are listed by name. Check their apps for specific cues before the design pass.

Draft structure. There are two references: **MTB (Myanma Tourism Bank, mtb.com.mm)** for the structure and local trust feel, and **Binance (binance.com)** for a bold hero, a product row, and trust badges. MTB's homepage is a trust-first local bank layout: a promo banner at the top, a grid of product cards with short bullet benefits, a live exchange-rate box, a licence and "about us" statement, a mobile banking feature list with "How to apply", and a contact-heavy footer. It mixes English and Arakanese copy.
pethar keeps its liquid glass look on top of that: frosted, translucent cards over a soft, deep gradient, with rounded corners and light edge highlights. Compared with Binance, it's calmer and more like a bank than a crypto exchange.

Audience: people and small businesses in Arakan. Copy is bilingual on the page, in English and Arakanese.

## 1. Hero (MTB promo banner + Binance-style focus)
- **Headline (Binance-style, one bold line, oversized type):** Your money, finally in your hands.
- **Arakanese line:** a short headline in Arakanese, shown under the English one.
- **Subhead:** One trusted app for your wallet, your bank, and crypto, built for Arakanese.
- **Call to action (Binance-style, a single prominent button, optionally with a phone-number field beside it):** Getpethar
- **Cash App-style tagline under the button:** "Send money with just a phone number or a pethar handle."
- **Quick-action chips (KBZPay / K PLUS / PayPay):** Scan to Pay, Send, Top-up, shown as small glass chips under the button.
- **Visual:** a wide glass banner that can rotate promos (as MTB does with its deposit promotions), with the phone mockup on the right.
- **Exchange-rate box (MTB-style):** a small glass widget showing today's AKD/USD exchange rates, with a "last updated" time. This uses live data only.

## 2. Security
- **Headline:** Safe like a bank. Simple like an app.
- **Points (short bullets, like MTB's product cards):**
  - Biometric login and a PIN on every transfer
  - Encryption, with keys kept on your device
  - An instant alert for every transaction, and freeze your account anytime
  - Fraud monitoring 24/7
- **Visual:** a row of glass tiles with icons.
- **Stripe-style trust panel:** a glass panel with system status/uptime, encryption standard, and compliance notes. Real figures only.
- **Trust badges (Binance-style):** a strip of glass badges, such as Licensed, Encrypted, 24/7 Support, and Verified ID. Show only claims that are true.

## 3. Privacy and policy (MTB licence statement + DBS/UOB/OCBC-style clarity)
- **Headline:** Licensed, transparent, and on your side.
- **Content:** a short statement of who runspethar, its licence or regulatory status, and where it's based. Use real, verified wording only.
- **Points:** we never sell your data, you control what you share, and our terms are in plain language.
- **Layout (DBS / UOB / OCBC-style):** short, scannable glass cards, one for each commitment, instead of a dense block of text.
- **Links:** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, KYC/AML Compliance.

## 4. Products (MTB card grid with a Binance product-row feel)
- **Headline:** Everything money, in one place.
- **Everyday tone (POSB / PayLah!):** warm, neighbourly copy for the wallet and savings cards.
- **Glass cards, each with 2–3 bullet benefits and a "More detail" link:**
  - **Wallet:** Send, receive, and pay with a QR code, even on slow connections.
  - **Banking / iBanking:** Savings and current accounts, transfers, bill payments, and statements, all without the queue.
  - **Crypto:** Buy, hold, and send digital assets, with clear prices and clear risk notes.
- **Wallet card copy (PayPal / Cash App tone):** "Send money to anyone, instantly." Each card gets one bold verb.
- **Layout:** one horizontal row of glass cards that can be swiped on mobile, with an icon, a one-line value, and a quick-action button on each (like Binance's product rows). The MTB-style bullets show on hover or when a card expands.
- Optional later cards: remittance (MTB highlights workers' remittance), and mobile top-up.

## 5. Mobile interface showcase (MTB feature list + KBZPay / K PLUS / Bangkok Bank app patterns)
- **Headline:** Banking 24/7, right from your phone.
- **Hero mockup (PayPay-style):** a large central Scan button for QR payments.
- **Phone mockups show the familiar local home screen:** a glass balance card at the top (Bangkok Bank / K PLUS), a quick-action row (Scan, Pay, Transfer, Top-up), and a service icon grid below it (KBZPay / TrueMoney).
- **Spending insights screen (DBS / UOB / OCBC):** a glass chart card showing monthly spending by category.
- **Feature list with screenshots:** Payment and Transfer, View and Download Statements, Mobile Top-Up, Crypto.
- **"How to get started" (MTB has "How to apply"):** 1) Download, 2) Verify your phone number and ID, 3) Start sending. No branch visit needed, which setspethar apart from MTB's register-at-a-branch flow.
- **Buttons:** Google Play, App Store, APK download.

## 6. Super app pitch (Alipay / KBZPay / TrueMoney)
- **Headline:** One app. Every part of your financial life.
- **Subhead:** Pay shops, top up your phone, send money home, and grow your savings, all in one place.
- **Mini-apps grid (Alipay-style):** glass tiles for bill pay, phone top-up, merchant payments, remittance, savings, and crypto, with "more coming" tiles.
- **Deals and rewards row (TrueMoney / PayPay-style):** points, cashback and partner merchant offers.
- **Promo strip (MTB-style campaign banner):** space for the current offer.
- **Call to action (repeated):** Getpethar

## 7. Footer (contact-heavy, like MTB)
- **Contact block:** support email, hotline number, and office address in Arakanese or Yangon. Use real details only.
- **Columns:** Products, Company (About, Careers), Support (Help Center, Fees, Exchange Rates).
- **Legal links:** Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Crypto Risk Disclosure, Complaints, Licensing information.
- **Trust badges repeated:** a small badge row above the legal links.
- **Also:** a language switcher (English / Arakanese), social icons, and ©pethar.
