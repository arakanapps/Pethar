# Péthar Enterprise Compliance & Regulatory Framework

## 1. Executive Summary

**Péthar (ဖေ့သာ)** is a self-sovereign digital cash infrastructure built for financial resilience. This document outlines our corporate compliance posture, stablecoin reserve auditing, and privacy standards.

---

## 2. Reserve Backing Standard (1 ₳ = $1.00 USD)

* **Parity Standard:** Every unit of **₳ (Arakan Dollar)** in user circulation is guaranteed 1:1 with audited US Dollar collateral.
* **Collateral Asset Allocation:**
  * **USDT (Tether USD):** Tier-1 treasury reserve.
  * **USDC (Circle Internet Financial):** Audited monthly attestation standard.
  * **USD1 (Regulated Stablecoin):** Institutional settlement reserve.
* **Multi-Signature Vaults:** Reserve movements require 3-of-5 hardware key approvals from designated treasury trustees.
* **Proof of Reserves:** Daily on-chain cryptographic attestations published to the public transparency ledger.

---

## 3. Privacy & Google Cloud Verification

* **Google API Services User Data Policy:** Péthar strictly adheres to Google's Limited Use requirements.
* **No Telemetry Selling:** User financial transaction data, identity records, and biometric metadata are never sold or rented to commercial brokers.
* **Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17):** Users retain the permanent right to delete their credentials, profiles, and associated records via `privacy@pethar.co.uk`.

---

## 4. Offline Humanitarian Self-Sovereignty

* **Sanctions & Freedom of Payment:** In scenarios of telecommunications shutdowns or infrastructure disruptions, Péthar's offline Cashu bearer tokens operate peer-to-peer over BLE and NFC.
* **No Unsolicited Data Collection:** The offline mode requests local hardware permissions (Bluetooth / NFC / Camera) only upon user-initiated send/receive actions.
